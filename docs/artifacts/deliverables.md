# Deliverables Artifact

`deliverables.json` lists the planned outputs implied by a creative brief.

Path:

```text
.byom-video/agent_plans/<agent_plan_id>/deliverables.json
```

Schema version:

- `openvfx_deliverables.v1`

Examples:

- edited video
- Instagram Reel
- social caption options
- narration or hook outline

The artifact describes expected outputs but does not render or export them.
